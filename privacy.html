隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 16 日。Happy Markdown 不收集任何数据。

应用收集的数据

没有。应用不含账号体系、分析统计、广告、跟踪标识与崩溃上报，也不读取设备的硬件标识。开发者无法从你的使用中获得任何信息。

文稿与设置存在哪里

  • 文稿是你自己选择的文件夹里的 .md 文件，应用只读写这些文件。
  • 设置在系统标准配置目录下，随应用卸载而移除。
  • 运行日志写在本机的系统日志目录，用于排查故障，菜单「帮助 → 打开日志文件夹」可以直接打开。日志不离开这台机器。

网络访问

应用默认不联网，仅在下列两种情形下发出请求，两者都由你自行配置：

  • AI 指向本机 Ollama 服务时，请求只发往本机地址 127.0.0.1:11434，不出这台电脑。
  • 你在设置里填写了自己的 API 地址时，相关文档内容会发往你填写的那个地址，由你选择的模型服务商处理，适用该服务商的隐私政策。

除此之外，应用不与任何服务器通信，也不存在开发者的服务端。

购买与退款

通过 App Store 完成的购买、付款与退款由 Apple 处理，该部分数据由 Apple 按其隐私政策收集，开发者不接触你的支付信息。

第三方共享

不共享。应用不向第三方发送任何数据。

儿童

应用不收集任何用户信息，因此不涉及儿童数据的收集与处理。

政策变更

政策如有变更，本页会同步更新并调整生效日期。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026. Happy Markdown collects no data.

Data the app collects

None. There is no account system, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking identifier and no crash reporting, and the app does not read hardware identifiers. The developer receives nothing about your use of the app.

Where documents and settings are stored

  • Your documents are .md files in a folder you choose; the app only reads and writes those files.
  • Settings live in the standard system configuration directory and are removed with the app.
  • A run log is written to the system log directory on your machine to help diagnose problems, reachable from Help → Open Log Folder. The log does not leave your computer.

Network access

The app is offline by default and makes requests in two cases only, both configured by you:

  • With AI assist pointing at a local Ollama service, requests go to 127.0.0.1:11434 on your own machine.
  • If you enter your own API endpoint in Settings, the related document content is sent to that endpoint and handled by the model provider you chose, under that provider's privacy policy.

Beyond these, the app contacts no server, and there is no server operated by the developer.

Purchases and refunds

Purchases, payments and refunds made through the App Store are handled by Apple, which collects that data under its own privacy policy. The developer never sees your payment details.

Sharing with third parties

None. The app sends no data to third parties.

Children

The app collects no user information at all, so no children's data is collected or processed.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page is updated and the effective date is revised.
